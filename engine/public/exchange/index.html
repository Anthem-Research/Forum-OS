How to play

Explore the fog. Claim land next to your territory. Build farms, forests, mines, workshops and homes. Trade with other towns to get what you need. Grow to 12 people and make two alliances for a prosperity victory, or bring all four towns into your network. Other towns expand. Recruit guards, send food to build trust, form alliances, or declare war. War stops trade. Guards need money or food each turn. Allies send supplies and help defend your land.

Barter needs a matching want. Shells are useful money but can be gathered on coasts. Gold is mined inland. Paper can be issued by your town, but trading partners may raise their prices. Bitcoin rewards follow a limited issuance schedule. All balances are fictional. These are simplified rules for a game, not predictions about actual currencies.

Tap a visible tile for actions. Select a scout or ship and tap an adjacent tile to move. Discover technologies using the Tech button. Sailing unlocks ports and ships; a port and ship unlock freight with Cove. Move the map by dragging. Every turn, food is eaten, goods are produced and caravans move. Goods bought from other towns arrive after one or more turns. Buying raises local prices; supplying demanded goods lowers demand. Workshops need wood and stone. Upgrade productive buildings with tools, or spend those tools recruiting guards.
